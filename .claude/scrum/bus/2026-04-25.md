### Three smoking guns (root cause of the $88 gap)

Also: ChatGPT provider's path at `chatgpt_provider.py:543` is `usage.get("completion_tokens", token_count)` — falls back to chunk count when OpenAI's streaming response doesn't include `usage` (which it won't unless `stream_options.include_usage=true` is set; couldn't verify quickly but the today-trace example showed `promptTokens: 0`, suggesting the same shape problem is happening for ChatGPT cost too).
